## Feature-complete first structural pass, not final visual-quality certification

This pass targets *all four* environmental systems together, rather than just adding façade decorations:

1. **Architectural variety** — seven possible seeded footprint setbacks, three extra muted district palettes, six alternate tower decoration profiles, layered cornices, window hoods, parapet patterns, optional pitched-looking roofline mass, rooftops with ventilation or utility rooms, balconies and drainpipes, shop identities, industrial ducts/skylights and workshop-front supports. Multi-storey stair circulation remains as before.
2. **Street-level detail** — cracked/patchy road surfaces, intersection stripes where no canal or tram crosses, sidewalk drainage grates, rare district-dependent outer-edge clutter and weathering. Street center and tram track placement remain protected.
3. **Environmental layering** — variation in block subdivision sizes, variable setbacks and corner yards, rooftop silhouettes, density changes within neighborhoods, different ground materials, weathered frontage. The terrain-relief and sewer+cavern network remain unchanged.
4. **Interior follow-through** — small non-destructive secondary fixtures, functional doorway access in new buildings, existing fully furnished shops/clinics/warehouses/factories/dwellings preserved (their current interior furniture remains generated by LotPainter).

## Abandoned and ruined architecture (added to Pass 12)

This is not a cosmetic decal layer. The original city generator now produces **whole derelict buildings** alongside occupied buildings and junkyards. The patterns are seeded per lot and restricted to new worlds with `world_detail_version=1`.

- **Occupied:** normal furnished, accessible structures. Not every building is ruined.
- **Neglected:** uneven cracked masonry, soot-darkened patchwork, rust/copper repairs, occasional leaking gutters and rooftop wear.
- **Boarded / abandoned:** some lower windows are boarded, shop signage says `CLOSED / ABANDONED`, broken wall segments, small piles of debris/cobwebs, unoccupied storefronts. Existing loot/furniture is preserved for scavenging.
- **Partial collapse:** a broken upper side wall with a small optional roof gap, plus damaged parapets and rooftop clutter. The north-wall stairwell, primary entrance and farmland/tank rooftops remain protected.
- **Ruined shell remnants:** some derelict rubble lots now have fragments of standing brick/deepslate walls (not just a flat gravel lot), leaving the center accessible.

**Habitat mix:** new-world derelict neighborhoods choose from rubble, junkyard, vacant, parks/nursery, tenements, warehouses, shops and factories. This makes the district recognizable and avoids repetitive uninterrupted rows of factories or repeated empty rubble fields. The numbered selections are *building condition probabilities among eligible buildings* and NOT percentage of land area.

**Verified deterministic condition distribution** (10 seeds × 101² sites per district, **714,070 decisions**):

| District | Boarded or partly collapsed | Partly collapsed only |
|---|---:|---:|
| Derelict | 64.98% | 27.74% |
| Outskirts | 28.69% | 7.97% |
| Industrial | 14.90% | 4.04% |
| Waterfront | 6.04% | 0% |
| Residential | 2.01% | 0% |
| Graveyard | 4.04% | 0% |
| Market | 0% | 0% |

**Safety:** no terrain, vanilla caves, ore or portal code changed for these ruins. Main entrances and northern upper-floor stairs have a protected buffer; abandoned storefronts do not spawn normal villager shopkeepers. Clinics, hospitals, start shelter, named landmarks, nurseries/greenhouses and ordinary farmland are explicitly excluded from destructive edits. Decorative debris can be mined normally. Chunk-spanning writes are clipped by Canvas, and ruined condition comes from a pure hash rather than chunk-processing order.

**Visual caveat:** this is a procedural first pass inspired by the scale of detail in hand-built Minecraft cities; it is not a claim of Greenfield-level hand-crafted fidelity or verified Minecraft screenshots. Actual rendered block states, sky visibility through holes, loot, collision, pathfinding and worldgen performance need live review. Particularly test whether storefront signs place correctly and whether the derelict skyline differs visibly from residential districts.

## CRITICAL: saved-world compatibility
`CitySettings.worldDetailVersion` is persisted in the world generator codec as optional `world_detail_version`: missing defaults to `0` (prior generation untouched). The new normal preset explicitly writes `1`. A fresh world is required to see consistent high-detail buildings and lots. Never flip the field in an existing save, as existing chunks can meet different building footprints/streets.

## Determinism and performance
- `WorldDetail` has no random RNG state; selections are derived solely from seed, coordinates, lot hash, and district.
- All writes are clipped to the current chunk through `Canvas`. Never use an unguarded `Canvas.get` outside the chunk.
- The new street finish is a low-cost per-column seed hash. Furniture pass iterates only 256 columns per chunk and places sparse fixtures at outer road-band edges.
- No change to deep Minecraft cave noise, mining, resources, or crop/farm generation. No extra custom blocks required; material choices are obtainable vanilla or existing mod blocks.
- Surface clutter must never block arterial centers, tram lines, sewer hatches, major building entrances, or navigation routes; test these *in game*.

### Mandatory visual/play checks (keep in accumulated test list)
- Start four fresh worlds and visit origin, 1 km north, industrial, derelict, waterfront, outskirts, markets; inspect at street level and from roofs.
- Compare >40 building faces: count distinct colors, footprints and rooflines; avoid uniform teal and repeated rectangular facades.
- Walk in/out of every building type, use stairs, beds, chests, furnaces, doors, roof farms; inspect wall signs, awnings, parapets, window hoods, balconies and utility rooms.
- Test that interiors are accessible and signs do not detach, roofs do not leak, windows remain windows, and balcony bars do not seal entrance.
- Walk trams, avenues, sidewalks, back alleys, slopes, canals and bridges; validate curb/wear/drainage placement, traffic and fixture spacing.
- Generate chunks in opposite orders and compare hashes; test across chunk and neighborhood boundaries, no cut-off facades/trees, no orphan street props.
- Confirm Glow Moth moving light cleans up, spawn eggs, all mobs, portals, sewer-to-natural-cave links, ore and surface resource availability.
- Stress test dedicated multiplayer and 8–16 chunk render distances; monitor TPS/GC and worldgen stalls; compare against Pass 11.
