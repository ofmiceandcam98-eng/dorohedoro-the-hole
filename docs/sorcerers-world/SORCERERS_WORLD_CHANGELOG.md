# Sorcerers' World: changelog

## 2026-11-26: settlements, first building library (branch `sorcerers-world/towns`)

Added:
- Structure type `dorohedoro:sorcerer_settlement`, with structure, structure set and biome tag data. It generates
  cities, towns and hamlets in the Sorcerers' World only, spaced at least about 300 blocks apart.
- `worldgen/sorcerer/`:
  - `TownPlanner`: a terrain-first road network with a plaza, thoroughfares, streets, alleys, dead ends and joins; then
    lots, lamps and walks; a viability downgrade for poor sites.
  - `RoadNet`, `TownFrame`, `TownCatalog`, `TownRandom`: the pure-Java pieces the planner and painters share.
  - `TownRoadsPiece`: grading, bridges, retaining walls, half-step slabs, lamps, the fountain or well, the bell.
  - `TownBuildingPiece`: walks with stairs, then the building.
  - `SorcererBuildings`, `TownArchitecture`, `TownPalette`, `Painter`, `WorldSink`: 15 original designs with interiors.
  - `TownLoot`: 11 loot tables, generated by `tools/gen-sorcerer-loot.mjs`.
- `qa/SorcererTownQa`, run inside the existing `HoleQa` server run. It checks:
  - registration and placement, and that settlements can only occur in the Sorcerers' World;
  - the share of land that is settled, and a biome census;
  - overlaps and buildings on roads;
  - door-to-road walks, room reachability, lighting, roofs and foundations;
  - road walkability, villagers, loot and signs;
  - settlement-scoped chunk hashes for determinism.
- `tools/qa-sorcerers.sh` (server QA in both orders plus the determinism comparison) and `tools/qa-sorcerer-planner.sh`
  (offline planner fixture).

Changed:
- `registry/ModWorldgen.register` also registers the settlement registries.
- `qa/HoleQa` adds the `sorcerer_settlements` section to its report.

Also in this pass (owner's direction, 2026-11-26):
- **New worlds use vanilla terrain for the Sorcerers' World:** `SorcererVanillaBiomeSource` wraps the vanilla multi-noise
  overworld biomes.
- `SorcererWeatherMixin`: no rain there.
- `SorcererStructureFilterMixin`: no vanilla villages or outposts there.
- `SettlementFeatureGuardMixin`: no lakes or boulders in settlements.
- `SettlementTidy`: no tree remnants over streets and houses.
- Settlements generate at `top_layer_modification`.
- The Hydra Forest advancement accepts dark forest in the Sorcerers' World.
- Release bumped to **BETA 0.4** (`0.4.0-beta`).

Not changed: the Hole generator, `world_detail_version`, portals and magic. The old Sorcerers' World biome source and
noise settings stay in the mod for saved worlds. Settlements appear only in newly generated Sorcerers' World chunks.
