# Sorcerers' World: structure catalog

All entries are original designs in `worldgen/sorcerer/SorcererBuildings.java`, placed by the settlement structure
`dorohedoro:sorcerer_settlement`. Coordinates are local to the building: x runs along the facade, z runs from the front
wall (z = 0) to the back, and y = 0 is the ground floor's feet level. The door is at (doorX, 0, 0) and the street side is
z = -1. A building may face any of the four cardinal directions (it turns to face its road). Footprint and placement
data are in `TownCatalog.SPECS`.

Lore class for every entry: **ORIGINAL EXPANSION**, built in a canon-inspired style (see the lore register). None of
them reproduces a specific building from the manga.

Permission status for every entry: original work, no third-party source. Integration status: generated by the
settlement structure. Test status: see `SORCERERS_WORLD_TEST_REPORT.md`.

**Palette:** the Exchange comes in two variants, chosen per city: Frit's original bright Victorian colours, or soot-dark.
The dark variant swaps yellow and orange terracotta for grey and brown, end stone for tuff, and clay for packed mud.
The template is placed through the painter, so it rotates to face its street and clips to the chunk like every other
building.

## Rotation and placement constraints

- A building faces the cardinal direction closest to its road and stands back 1 to 6 blocks (3 to 7 for wealthy
  homes). A walk of 1 to 8 cells, with stairs at every rise, runs from the door to the paving.
- Footprints never overlap each other, the roads, the verges, lamps or other walks, and they keep a gap of at least one
  block from each other.
- The terrain rise across a footprint must not exceed the type's max slope. Foundations then fill to natural ground and
  the site is excavated above the floor.
