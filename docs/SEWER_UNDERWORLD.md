# Sewer–Cave Hybrid: Hole Underground (2026-10-31)

## Final user requirement

**The Hole must feel like a city with actual sewer infrastructure, and it must still support normal Minecraft mining.** Not every sewer ends in a cave. Most passages remain ordinary brick-lined drains, and some have closed inspection rooms or flood cisterns. A sizeable minority of long exploratory branches eventually lead into natural rock, caves and huge caverns, but most junctions still remain sewer-only. The change must never punish players who prefer normal digging, ore hunting, cave exploring, minecart networks or normal survival progression.

## Active generation (new worlds only)

- New worlds select `dorohedoro:hole_sewers` with `city.sewer_underworld=true`, leaving existing save generator settings unchanged.
- **Preserved natural Minecraft cave noise below Y=24**: the original `hole.json` final density and initial density are selected under this threshold using valid `minecraft:range_choice` density functions. Vanilla ore veins, deep mining, caverns, bedrock, deepslate, subterranean structures and aquifers remain enabled. Caves are not deleted or replaced with solid stone everywhere.
- **Shallow infrastructure stays constructed**: above Y=24, the noise settings maintain solid foundations. Deliberate municipal sewer openings and storm cisterns are carved explicitly. Random cheese caves do not suddenly open behind upper sewer walls or into urban basements.
- **Municipal sewer arteries**, around nominal Y=49–54: linear brick tunnels with central drains and walkable sides. Not all have side branches.
- **Mid-depth service conduits**, around Y=29–36: constructed dry walkways and water runnels; access shafts from the streets.
- **Lower mine galleries** at nominal Y=12 and -36: playable routes with 2-block-deep masonry shell that separate normal galleries from wild caves except at intentional transitions.
- **Closed sewer-only rooms** at approximately 18% of intersections: short brick passages and end-capped inspection bays with working, loot-table-based salvage barrels. These remain sewers and do not open into natural caves.
- **Cave-leading branches** at approximately 30% of intersections in newly generated worlds: begin beside the deep gallery, follow 19 blocks of intact sewer masonry, then 12 blocks of ruined masonry, and then a long deepslate/mining passage with a walkable stepped descent. At roughly 59 blocks along the branch, the player emerges into an irregular carved rock cavern. Vanilla deep caves also remain generated, but connection to any separate naturally generated cave is not yet guaranteed or runtime-verified. Main infrastructure is never an immediate open cave.
- **Other intersections** (roughly 58%) have no cave-leading or inspection branch. Cistern sites remain separate, and utility shafts stay protected.
- One demonstration cave branch is guaranteed at arterial crossing `(0,0)` for in-game discovery and QA, but reaching it requires finding the underground gallery first.
- Extra lower excavation halls provide variety. **Ore generation, resource drops, mining, tools, crafting, natural caverns and ordinary Minecraft gameplay are mandatory.** Natural caves may have incidental additional intersections with mining or world structures; the deliberately lined galleries reduce that risk.
- The original `dorohedoro:hole` noise settings file is kept unchanged for existing saves, as is `CitySettings.DEFAULT` (`sewer_underworld=false`). The `sewer_cave_frequency` field is saved in each world generator: new default worlds explicitly use `30`, while existing saves without this field decode to legacy frequency `9`, which selects the **exact original** 1-in-11 layout and does not change previously generated branches. These new settings are encoded in worldgen, not a live config. The ability to switch an already-generated world to this variant without seams is NOT supported.
- Sewers use the reusable `dorohedoro:sewer_brickwork` block: mineable, drops itself, original 16x16 texture, working item model and a survival crafting recipe generated by `ModRecipes`. Salvage uses the registered `dorohedoro:chests/sewer_cache` loot table.

These are **gameplay design extensions**, not claims about a canonical Dorohedoro sewer map.

## Increased cave access (2026-10-31 update)

- New Hole world preset: **30%** cave-leading service branches, **18%** sealed inspection rooms, and **52%** ordinary sewer intersections (targets; hash sampling causes minor variance).
- Measured across 146,410 intersections and ten seeds: **29.89% / 17.99% / 52.06%** respectively. The previous profile measured **9.07% / 18.16% / 72.77%**.
- The route does **not** turn into a cave immediately: about 19 blocks of intact sewer, another 12 blocks of ruined masonry, then deep stone passage; the cavern mouth remains approximately 59 blocks from the starting junction.
- Each cave route still begins off the lower service gallery; main street sewers remain municipal infrastructure and deep natural cave noise and Minecraft ores remain accessible by ordinary mining.
- A new generator field `sewer_cave_frequency` stores the selection frequency with the world. Older saves without that field retain the exact original 1-in-11 selection. To see the new 30% distribution throughout the city, start a **new world**; do not edit saved generator settings mid-world.
- This is a change to the **selection frequency**, not proof of 30% guaranteed physically reachable existing cave systems. Client and server testing remain required.

## Cavern geometry upgrade (new saved preset version 1)

- `city.sewer_cavern_version: 1` is selected by the new-world preset. Existing saved generators that omit this flag default to version `0` and keep their old tunnels/room layouts.
- In v1, most endpoints are modest chambers (14–17 by 14–18 block horizontal radius). Around one in six are broad chambers (20–24 by 21–25 block horizontal radius).
- V1 adds a one-block-at-a-time descending, supported entry from the old deep gallery to the cavern center, including relief-adjusted Y so terrain height changes cannot create unplanned multi-block drops.
- The 19-block intact sewer, 12-block damaged sewer and natural-stone approach are unchanged; this does not make any sewer immediately transition to a cavern.
- No extra ore veins or resource gating were introduced. Normal vanilla mining and cave generation remain enabled.
- Detailed verification and limitations: `docs/SEWER_CAVERN_TRAVERSAL.md`.

## Verification boundary

Offline JSON checks, Java syntax checks and deterministic policy tests are useful; only an actual NeoForge `runData`, `build`, client launch and multiplayer server test can confirm the registered dimensions and cave system work in the game. See `docs/SEWER_TEST_RESULTS.md`.

## Required Windows testing

1. Back up existing saves. Do not test changes in an old world; make a fresh default Hole world.
2. Run, sequentially: `node tools/make-textures.mjs`; `./gradlew --no-daemon runData`; `./gradlew --no-daemon build`. **Do not launch two Gradle instances in parallel.** Fix compile/data errors if present.
3. Launch the client. Find a sewer hatch and inspect several lengths of ordinary brick-lined sewer. It should **not** become a cave after a few blocks.
4. Walk to closed inspection rooms and flood-control cisterns. Confirm they remain visibly constructed, have a safe floor, workstations/loot as present, and no accidental cave holes.
5. Descend to a lower gallery. Follow a rare cave branch through intact sewer, damaged sewer, stone passage and **finally** natural cavern, in that order. Verify climbability both directions, lighting and hazards. For reproducibility there is one selected at xLine(0)/zLine(0), offset x+12.
6. Verify **ordinary naturally shaped deep caves still exist** away from the city tunnels, and mine stone, coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, diamonds and other vanilla minerals with ordinary tools.
7. Check underground resources, strongholds, mineshafts, trial chambers, lava, water and safe portals remain accessible; track any significant change in find rates relative to normal Minecraft.
8. Run `tools/qa-world.sh` for seed 12345 in forward and reverse order (radius >=12), compare, and repeat across ten representative seeds.
9. Test surface and sewer chunk borders, entity spawning, save/restart, and high-load multiplayer cave exploration.
10. Capture street-to-sewer-to-cavern screenshots. Mark all unverified features honestly until those checks pass.

## Current limits

The new cave branches are deterministic voxel shapes and deliberately minimal. Improved debris props, sewer-themed mobs, more room variants, minecart servicing and further environmental detail remain separate future work. Most importantly, **do not re-disable natural caves in a future optimization pass**. For Minecraft compatibility, ordinary survival must always remain possible.
