# The Hole — accepted visual & gameplay contract (Pass52)

1. Geography derives from Minecraft's world seed and climate, not a required single central-city biome. The Hole can cover snowfields, dead taiga, desert/dust, badlands and mountain edges, and these climate regions may intersect occupied city blocks.
2. Urban density and biome are separate. Dense blocks, neighborhoods, derelict buildings and open terrain may occur within **any** climate; fully formed downtown is not mandatory in every climate or on every start.
3. Every climate must remain recognizably The Hole where inhabited: harsh, filthy, scarred infrastructure, improvised brick/concrete/metal construction, strange but purposeful businesses, cramped non-grid streets and activity.
4. Outer areas may include small Hole-style settlements, ruined services, farms, mines, dead trees and open survival terrain. Do not create full duplicate downtowns in every province or turn all wilderness into empty vanilla land.
5. Actual Minecraft survival remains central: craftable and usable furnishings; progression, food, wood, ore, caves, rivers, farming, natural hostiles and mining. No chest-lined streets, brightly lit open countryside or decorative fake floors.
6. Existing priority order: city/world visual quality, irregular streets and usable decorated individual rooms FIRST; environmental poisoning/Ash-Snow/Sludge-Ice/toxic snowballs/Cross-Eye camps later; major villager/faction AI and trades only after world quality approval.
7. New generation behaviors are opt-in per world via versioned settings and biome-source codec flags. Never silently regenerate old worlds or change already generated chunks; always run current AND legacy tests.
8. Always compare real Minecraft screenshots against official Dorohedoro manga/anime references. Distinguish conceptual references from actual implemented images. Source-only work is never called a Minecraft-tested release.
