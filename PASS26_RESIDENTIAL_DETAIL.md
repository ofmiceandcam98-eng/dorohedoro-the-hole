# Pass 26 — Procedural residence furnishing / lighting

Based on the source work already present in Pass 25. Enhances compact houses and larger apartments with additional functional kitchenette fixtures, washstands, shelves, rugs, small furniture and secondary ceiling lanterns. Seeded by building identity, storey and room coordinates. New generator preset defaults to v15; old v14 saves retain their earlier logic.

## Reference direction
- Tokyo City Mid-rise Apartment Building (Planet Minecraft): room-scale zoning and inhabited living areas.
- Minecraft.net Interior Motives: coordinated exterior and interior purpose.
- Minecraft Wiki Building a metropolis: compact residential zoning, varied housing scale and deliberate lighting.

## Limitations
- Not compiled with NeoForge or tested in game.
- Does not import or replicate reference maps.
- Furnishing still uses vanilla blocks, not bespoke furniture models.
- A sample build and night-time interior inspection are required before approving visual quality.
