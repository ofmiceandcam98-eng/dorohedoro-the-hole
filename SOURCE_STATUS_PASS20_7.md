# Dorohedoro: The Hole — Pass 20.7 / Actual Limb Severing

**NOT COMPILED. NOT TESTED IN MINECRAFT.** No Gradle invocation or dependency download was attempted.

Baseline: `Dorohedoro_Pass20_6_Full_Source_3D_Technique_Meshes_NOT_COMPILED.zip`. Source layout unchanged; complete project, not patch-only.

## Behavior added

- All eight Dismemberment techniques still deal their original health damage and impose the relevant wound, **but wounds are now applied only if actual damage registers**. This prevents an invulnerable target suffering a limb loss from a failed impact. There is only one limb-loss roll per affected target per cast (the old double-application path for Severing Wave is removed).
- Four advanced cutting techniques can remove an arm/leg from a survival/adventure **player** after a successful hit. One maximum limb removed per hit. Creative/Spectator players cannot be amputated. Probability increases with the mastery of that particular technique and is rolled exclusively on the server.
- A missing limb is tracked as a persistent server-side four-bit mask, with separate left/right arm/leg flags. It is retained across relog and vanilla player death/respawn in the same character, until regenerated by sufficiently mastered Healing. Severed limbs impose stronger attack/movement penalties. A player can survive even after losing multiple limbs, but will have severe mobility/combat limitations.
- Nearby players receive an authenticated, server-to-client mask packet on changes and when they begin tracking an injured player. Third-person vanilla body + sleeves/pants limbs are hidden. First-person missing arm and its held item are hidden as well. Reptile overlay arms are hidden accordingly. The server also periodically resends active missing-limb masks to cope with load timing.
- Normal Healing can clear temporary Dismemberment wound markers without automatically restoring a missing limb. Advanced regenerative Healing restores the missing limb mask and its attribute penalties; such repair is permanent (not a timed buff).
- Animated black smoke particles accompany the severing hit; existing three-dimensional slashes and healing meshes remain available.

### Actual loss chances (single successful eligible hit)

| Technique | First eligible mastery | Chance at threshold | Chance at mastery 1000 | Eligible limb |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Clean Cut | 350 | 1.5% | 6.05% | Left or right leg |
| Dismember Strike | 400 | 5% | 14.6% | Left or right arm |
| Severing Wave | 550 | 1.5% | 5.55% | Left or right leg, per damaged player |
| Anatomy Rend | 600 | 10% | 22% | Any available arm or leg |

Other Dismemberment techniques inflict wounds and health damage, without limb-loss rolls.

### Regeneration gates

- **Self:** Mend Flesh mastery 800; Rapid Regeneration mastery 850. Purify Blood treats injuries but does not regenerate limbs.
- **Targeted ally:** Mend Ally mastery 750; Cleanse Ally mastery 900.
- **Group:** Lifeweave mastery 650; Restorative Field mastery 850.
- Regeneration only restores the missing-limb state and health/attributes; the original innate magic type, learned skills, and loadouts never change.

## Limitations / required testing

- The disappearance uses vanilla player model-part visibility, not a new animated detachable limb entity or ragdoll. Armor models, held-item third-person layers and modded replacement player models may render inconsistently; assess this in game before public release.
- Actual collision/locomotion remains vanilla dimensions, with significant server-owned movement penalties rather than bespoke hopping/crawling animation.
- Mobs still receive Dismemberment injuries but **actual missing-limb model modification is implemented for players only**.
- Multiplayer QA must test late tracking, first/third person, arm and leg hiding with clothing/armor, server restarts, die/respawn, heal/regrow, immunity frames, PvP restrictions and persistence.
- No vanilla Minecraft or NeoForge Java compilation was performed; API and runtime integrations are **unverified**.
