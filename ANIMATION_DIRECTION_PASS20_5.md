# Animation Direction — Eight Innate Magic Types

**Every Sorcerer produces dark smoke.** At release it is dirty black/charcoal, and each type is identified by what that smoke *causes*, not by a rainbow color.

| Innate magic | New animated components | Mechanics kept from earlier passes | Future art work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mushroom | Soot plumes -> organic fungal spores -> growth site rings | Real vanilla mushroom blocks, solid giant caps, permanent at high mastery | Original stylized growth mesh and mycelium crawling onto targets |
| Reptile | Ascending scale bands, claw traces, body-wrapping black smoke | Wall-climbing and larger collision scale | Rigged head/body morph with Ebisu-like uncanny silhouette |
| Dismemberment | Facing-aware razor-thin smoke seams and slash marks | Direct health damage, arm/leg/body injury state | Directional 3D slicing mesh and accurate wound decals |
| Healing | Smoke shroud -> ivory fiber closing/stitching at target | Can heal other players; repairs Dismemberment injuries | Body-surface skin repair and wound-closing rig animation |
| Curse | Coiling smoky tendrils, marks and terminal decay | Direct damage and hostile timed curses | Distinct body-clinging curse creature for late-game techniques |
| Protection | Dense layered smoke cocoon and temporary solid barrier construction | Real collision, temporary expiry | 3D shell growth and responsive impact deformation |
| Time | Smoke-bound, caster-facing dial with moving hands | Temporary slowing fields and damage | Mechanical apparition and precise distortion shader |
| Destruction | Compacted soot, concussive traveling impact rings, heat flecks | Real damage to PvP players and structures by mastery | Unique rolling blast fronts and destructible smoke shockwave geometry |

## Animation patterns (all 64 mapped)

- **Bolt:** directional curling smoke connects caster and target.
- **Touch:** localized, sharply defined impact on targeted body or surface.
- **Area:** ground-level expanding black smoke rings around affected space.
- **Body:** rising smoke coils trace the caster's silhouette.
- **Shell:** smoke wraps a living subject.
- **Construct:** smoke outlines real growth or a temporary collision barrier.
- **Rupture:** intense centered shockwave for explosive magic.

These are technique presentation profiles, not additional learnable magic types. The source remains deliberately independent of Tensura, Iron's Spellbooks, Ars Nouveau, GeckoLib and other addons.
